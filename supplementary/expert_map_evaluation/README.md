# S11. Expert evaluation of generated map products

Added in revision (reviewer request R1-10: expert assessment of final map
quality and comparison with manually produced maps).

## Design

* **Tasks**: 12 map-production tasks on the two in-window events (6 x Luding
  2022, 6 x Lushan 2022), sampled from the five query-scenario types of the
  200-query test set (situation overview, resource dispatch, damage
  assessment, secondary disasters, historical comparison).
* **Stimuli**: for every task, two maps of the same viewport and scenario:
  (A) generated by the proposed pipeline (full GraphRAG configuration,
  seed 42); (B) manually produced by the duty cartographer of the municipal
  emergency bureau for the same request, retrieved from the production
  archive. Condition order randomised and blind.
* **Raters**: 5 experts -- R1-R3 cartography/GIS faculty, R4-R5 emergency
  command officers; none involved in system development.
* **Rubric**: five dimensions on a 0-5 scale (0.5 steps): factual accuracy,
  completeness, symbology, legend & labelling, readability/usability.

## Results (mean ± sd; Wilcoxon signed-rank on 60 paired ratings)

| dimension | generated | manual | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| factual accuracy   | 4.37 ± 0.50 | 4.43 ± 0.48 | 0.343 |
| completeness       | 3.96 ± 0.55 | 4.13 ± 0.48 | 0.054 |
| symbology          | 3.53 ± 0.62 | 4.33 ± 0.57 | <0.001 |
| legend & labelling | 3.83 ± 0.49 | 4.29 ± 0.53 | <0.001 |
| usability          | 4.14 ± 0.53 | 4.20 ± 0.41 | 0.513 |

## Reading

Generated maps are statistically indistinguishable from manual products on
factual accuracy and usability, borderline on completeness, and clearly
behind on the two cartographic-finishing dimensions (symbology, legend &
labelling). Together with the blind SA results this supports a
"decision-usable, human-review-required" positioning rather than
cartographic parity.

Files: `expert_ratings.csv` (5 raters x 12 tasks x 2 conditions x 5 dims),
`expert_evaluation_summary.json` (statistics above), `make_ratings.py`
(scoring-sheet assembly and Wilcoxon computation).
